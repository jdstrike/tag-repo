# Portfolio

The Adecco Group portfolio organises around four pillars covering the entire workforce lifecycle. 25 services in total, delivered across Adecco, LHH, and Akkodis.

## The four pillars

| # | Pillar | Services | Focus |

| 3 | [Workforce strategy](/business-context/02-portfolio/pillar-3-workforce-strategy.md) | 5 | Planning the workforce ahead of demand. |
| 4 | [Transform and evolve](/business-context/02-portfolio/pillar-4-transform-evolve.md) | 5 | Engineering the technology and operating model that turns plans into outcomes. |
