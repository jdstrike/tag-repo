# Delivery models

Four ways the Adecco Group delivers, from the high-street branch to the digital self-service channel.

* [Branch network](/business-context/05-delivery-models/branch-network.md), The branch network is Adecco's capillary delivery base, blending modern recruiting tech with human contact across cities and rural towns.
* [Onsite solutions](/business-context/05-delivery-models/onsite-solutions.md), Onsite is the most complete delivery channel: a mini recruitment agency on the client's premises with 24/7 cover, dedicated HR experts, and end-to-end accountability from sourcing to health and safety.
* [Global hub](/business-context/05-delivery-models/global-hub.md), Global Hubs are TAG's nearshore and offshore delivery centres for multi-country recruiting at scale.
* [Digital Adecco](/business-context/05-delivery-models/digital-adecco.md), Digital Adecco is the zero-touch self-service channel built on QAPA and Adia, with proprietary AI-ML matching and 24/7 availability.
