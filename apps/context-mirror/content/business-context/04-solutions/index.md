# Solutions

The canonical solution set, mapped from the four-pillar portfolio.

* [Flexible placement](/business-context/04-solutions/flexible-placement.md), Flexible Placement is Adecco's classic temp staffing service delivered through three modes (Branch, Onsite, Master Vendor) plus digital self-service via QAPA and Adia.
* [Outsourcing (master)](/business-context/04-solutions/outsourcing.md), Adecco Outsourcing externalises whole processes and functions, taking accountability for output, KPI, and SLA across FSM, L&M, BPO, customer services, and HR services.
* [Outsourcing portfolio catalogue](/business-context/04-solutions/outsourcing-portfolio-catalogue.md), The Portfolio Catalogue is the canonical 163-slide internal master that maps Adecco's full offer in a 5+5+5 architecture: five Solutions, five Channels, and five priority Industries.
* [Outsourcing solution features](/business-context/04-solutions/outsourcing-solution-features.md), The Solution Features menu is the granular feature inventory across all five Adecco solutions, with explicit Shared vs Adecco-overall responsibility flags that anchor SOW-level contract design.
* [Outsourcing case library](/business-context/04-solutions/outsourcing-case-library.md), The Outsourcing Case Library is a 311-slide internal collection of cases across 27 countries, paired with a Q1 2022 digital case studies set covering eight digital products.
* [Permanent recruitment](/business-context/04-solutions/permanent-recruitment.md), Permanent Recruitment is one of the six Adecco solutions in the 2026 sales deck, scaling from dedicated single hire to mass recruitment.
* [Adecco Career Center](/business-context/04-solutions/career-center.md), ACC, the Adecco Career Center, is the centralised delivery channel for mass recruitment, recurring volume, and highly specialised roles.
