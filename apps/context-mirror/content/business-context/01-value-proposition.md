# Value proposition

## Brief

TAG is positioned as the global Workforce Readiness Partner for a world reshaped by AI, demographics, and skill scarcity, delivered through Adecco, Akkodis, and LHH under a single integrated portfolio. The Adecco Group voice is the four binding tone-of-voice pairs (clear & credible, inclusive & approachable, energetic & optimistic, curious & courageous) and a writing rhythm: to the point, punchy, active, confident and clear.6B EUR revenue, 60+ countries, 5,000+ offices, and 100,000+ clients per year.

## The four-pillar mental model

TAG organizes its go-to-market around four levers that map directly to client problems. Define workforce strategy is the cross-GBU advisory layer for CHROs and CFOs, covering planning, analytics, regulatory, and benchmarks. Attract, hire, and deploy is owned by Adecco and Pontoon, with executive search, flexible jobs, permanent hire, RPO, BPO, MSP, and inclusion staffing for COOs and procurement leaders. Skill, develop, and transition is LHH territory: workforce transformation, leadership development, reskilling, career transition, and executive solutions for restructuring or AI-driven role redesign. Transform and evolve is Akkodis country: product and system development, technical consulting, R&D outsourcing, AI and data at scale, and industry-specific engineering for CTOs and CIOs running build programs.

## Key facts

* 23.6B EUR revenue, 60+ countries, 5,000+ offices, 100,000+ clients per year
* 2M+ associates on assignment and 180k permanent placements per year
* 850k+ people trained or coached, 500k+ successfully career-transitioned
* 39k+ employees plus 30k+ specialist tech experts
* Brand architecture: Adecco for workforce, Akkodis for engineering, LHH for talent development
* Four differentiators: trust and integrity, innovation and sustainability, human centric, glo-cal strength
* Challenger Sales structure: trends, problems, levers, portfolio, stories
