# Selling models

Six ways the Adecco Group goes to market for workforce, recruitment, and AI-and-human delivery.

## Models

* [MSP, Pontoon](/business-context/03-selling-models/msp-pontoon.md), Pontoon is TAG's specialist MSP brand that runs contingent workforce, services procurement, and direct sourcing programs for global enterprises.
* [RPO, Pontoon](/business-context/03-selling-models/rpo-pontoon.md), RPO at TAG is delivered through three vehicles: Pontoon for global programs, LHH Recruitment Solutions for professional and executive layers, and Adecco branches for volume permanent.
* [Vendor Management System](/business-context/03-selling-models/vms.md), VMS is the web platform that runs contingent workforce orders, suppliers, and contracts.
* [Talent Supply Chain](/business-context/03-selling-models/tsc.md), Talent Supply Chain is Adecco's next-generation delivery model for multi-country recruiting, combining three Global Hubs, in-country hubs, and a proprietary AI engine.
* [Direct sourcing](/business-context/03-selling-models/direct-sourcing.md), Direct Sourcing at TAG is the Pontoon-managed approach to building a client-owned talent pool with curated freelancers and direct candidates, integrated into the contingent workforce supply chain.
* [rPotential, Salesforce JV](/business-context/03-selling-models/rpotential-jv.md), rPotential is a separate enterprise-intelligence company born from a joint venture between Adecco Group and Salesforce, with a hard contractual fence around co-branding.
