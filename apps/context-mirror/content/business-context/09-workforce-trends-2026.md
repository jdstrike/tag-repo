# Workforce Trends 2026

## Brief

Adecco's Workforce Trends 2026 Report polled 37,500 workers and 2,000 C-suite executives across 31 countries in 2025. It is the macro anchor for C-level pitches, organised into four chapters (Workforce Strategy, Competing for Top Talent, Upskilling and Mobility, Talent Evolution) with 21 country profiles and 7 industry profiles. The headline shift: the Great Resignation is over, salary is back in the top 3 reasons to stay, and AI is now the top trend for both workers and leaders. 87% of workers are willing to flex for AI (up from 79% in 2024) and 71% say their AI knowledge runs ahead of employer training. Future-ready workers jumped from 11% to 37%, driven mainly by tech savviness rather than adaptability or proactivity, leaving a clear gap to close. Flexibility is asymmetric: leaders have 1.2x more remote-working expectation while junior workers have half the schedule control of leaders. ## Key facts

* 37,500 workers and 2,000 C-suite executives surveyed across 31 countries (2025)
* Salary back in top 3 reasons to stay for the first time in four years
* 87% of workers willing to flex for AI (up from 79% in 2024)
* 71% of workers say their AI knowledge runs ahead of employer training
* Future-ready workers: 37% in 2026 vs 11% in 2024
* 21 country profiles and 7 industry profiles
* Industries covered include Technology, Financial and Professional Services, Mobility and Manufacturing, Life Sciences, Energy, Consumer Goods, Aerospace and Defence
* Material gap: 4th component of the Future-Ready model not unambiguously extracted
