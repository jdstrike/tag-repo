# GDM operating model

Global Delivery Model. The performance, governance, compliance, and tech-platform substance behind every TAG outsourcing deal.

* [KPIs, SLAs, reporting](/business-context/06-gdm-operating-model/kpis-slas-reporting.md), TAG's standard SLA, KPI, and reporting set is the performance backbone of every outsourcing or MSP contract, from response time to supplier scorecards.
* [Cost savings](/business-context/06-gdm-operating-model/cost-savings.md), TAG's cost savings story splits hard cost management (directly measurable) from soft cost management (indirect), with four levers: process improvement, assignment transition and conversions, turnover management, and demand and volume management.
* [Compliance](/business-context/06-gdm-operating-model/compliance.md), TAG's Global Integrity and Compliance Program runs out of Switzerland with country-level legal departments, annual code of conduct training, and a 24-hour ACE whistleblowing hotline.
* [Diversity and inclusion](/business-context/06-gdm-operating-model/diversity-and-inclusion.md), TAG's Inclusion and Wellbeing framework runs on three pillars (equity by design, lifelong employability, partnering for impact) and is anchored in zero tolerance, six global memberships, and Adecco Inclusion as a sourcing service in 25+ countries.
* [Health and safety](/business-context/06-gdm-operating-model/health-and-safety.md), TAG's health and safety model handles the structural challenge of not controlling the client's workplace through a four-tier responsibility chain plus tools like the Client Site Safety Evaluation, Top Five Safety Knows, and Job Servicing Guidelines.
* [Data protection and security](/business-context/06-gdm-operating-model/data-protection-and-security.md), Adecco runs a Global Data Protection Program since 2017 with a Global DPO and country-level privacy leads, GDPR-aligned operations, and a three-tier IT policy framework.
* [Global governance](/business-context/06-gdm-operating-model/global-governance.md), TAG runs premium accounts on a three-layer governance model: Global Account Director for strategy, QBRs, and escalation; Country POC for processes and commercial implementation; Local Account Manager for delivery.
* [Business continuity](/business-context/06-gdm-operating-model/business-continuity.md), TAG's crisis management and business continuity framework is run by a Global Crisis Operations Team backed by 60+ country-level COTs and the RACER workflow (Report, Assess, Convene, Execute, Resolve).
* [Payroll](/business-context/06-gdm-operating-model/payroll.md), TAG's Global Payroll capability processes 2 million payslips per month across all Adecco markets, run as a three-pillar model (Governance, Delivery, Associates Care) with zero compromises on compliance.
* [Pixid tech platform](/business-context/06-gdm-operating-model/pixid-tech-platform.md), Pixid is TAG's VMS platform of choice for European markets, anchored in a long-term partnership and delivering a complete module stack with ISO 27001 compliance and a BI layer for client-specific KPIs.
