# Workforce Solutions Ecosystem

## Brief

SIA estimates the global Workforce Solutions Ecosystem 2024 at 8.7T USD across six sectors, providing the canonical market-sizing reference for C-suite pitches. Adecco operates in five of the six sectors, with Pontoon as the MSP leader, LHH for HR services and outplacement, Akkodis for SOW consulting and direct hire in tech, and Adecco for staffing volume.

## Key facts

* Total Workforce Solutions Ecosystem 2024: 8.7T USD
* Six sectors: Direct Work Engagement (7.8T), Process Outsourcing (292B), HR Services and Other (201B), Staffing (200B), Payrolling and Compliance (104B), Talent Acquisition Tech (82B)
* RPO market: 6.9B USD
* MSP market: 6.8B USD
* Temp staffing: 104B USD net revenue (618B gross)
* Pontoon manages $14B in spend vs the 6.8B USD MSP net-revenue market (different dimensions: spend under management vs provider net revenue)
* SIA = Staffing Industry Analysts, founded 1989, Mountain View and London
