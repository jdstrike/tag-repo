The five lockup variants

Each variant lives on a background it's built for. Don't mix and match.

SVG

Default. White or near-white backgrounds.

Graphic devices

The two visual devices that travel with the brand. Use sparingly; less is more.

Unity gradient bar

Always at the bottom of a layout. Never the top. Never recoloured. Never resized in height.

Unity sphere

Cover slide, divider, hero accent. Never as a background fill.

The dont's

The brand book is the binding reference; this is the highlight reel.

Don't

Recolour, rotate, distort, recreate, or apply effects. Don't use the symbol alone without the wordmark. Don't use the family lockup without the keyline. Don't put colour-positive on dark backgrounds, or colour-negative on light. Don't use a turquoise keyline on the family lockup.
