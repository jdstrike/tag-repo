Executive summary

Three sentences, three signals, one decision.

1. The agility advantage is what we create when talent and technology operate on the same brief.

2. Three FY26 signals tell us we've reached it: Premium Client Tracker live for Microsoft, brand context layer at tag.schatt.me, SparkAI writing in TAG voice automatically.

3. The decision: scale the layer, or maintain it as a Group asset and let GBUs adopt at their own pace.

Two-column body text

Live

Across Adecco, Akkodis, and LHH we deliver end-to-end talent and technology solutions in more than 60 markets, combining global scale with local connection to deliver tailored, human-centred outcomes.

The agility advantage is what we create. Talent and technology, deployed together, against a real workforce problem. We make the future work for everyone.

SparkAI administers our Responsible AI Principles. Every output must satisfy ethical, human-centric, transparent, safe, and lawful standards before it ships.

The brand context layer at tag.schatt.me is the runtime substrate that makes all of this addressable by any AI tool the Group uses.
