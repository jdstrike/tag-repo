THE ADECCO GROUP, BUSINESS CONTEXT (v1.0.0, 2026-05-05)

WHO WE ARE (FY25)
The Adecco Group is your single strategic partner across the full talent and technology cycle: workforce, development, transformation, unified. €23.1 bn FY25 revenue (Adecco €18.5 bn, Akkodis €3.3 bn, LHH €1.3 bn). 169,000 FTEs incl. tech experts and bench. 60+ countries, 5,000 offices. 2 M+ associates per year, 100K+ clients, 870K+ people up- or reskilled in 2025, NPS 48. We make the future work for everyone by creating the agility advantage. In a world where agility is the only sustainable advantage, whoever controls talent, leadership, and technology drives the future.

THE FOUR-PILLAR MENTAL MODEL
Pillar 1, Attract, hire, deploy: bringing the right people in.
Pillar 2, Skill, develop, transition: growing them once they're in.
Pillar 3, Workforce strategy: planning the workforce ahead of demand.
Pillar 4, Transform and evolve: engineering the technology and operating model that turns plans into outcomes.

PORTFOLIO, 25 SERVICES ACROSS THE FOUR PILLARS
Pillar 1: Executive Search, Flexible Jobs, Permanent Hire, Recruitment Process Outsourcing (RPO), Business Process Outsourcing (BPO), Managed Service Provider (MSP) and MVP, Recruitment Solutions, Diversity and Inclusion staffing.
Pillar 2: Workforce Transformation, Leadership Development and Coaching, Reskilling and Upskilling, Career Transition and Mobility (Outplacement), Executive Solutions, Adecco Training and Academies, Assessment.
Pillar 3: Workforce Planning, Workforce Analytics, Salary Benchmarks, Regulatory Expertise, Process Optimization.
Pillar 4: Product and System Development, Technical Consulting, Research and Development Outsourcing, AI, Data and Automation at scale (Akkodis Intelligence), Industry-specific engineering.

THREE GBUs
Adecco (€18.5 bn), workforce solutions, AI agents and humans working as one. Workforce agility, guaranteed. From supply chain to agentic AI.
LHH (€1.3 bn), advisory and talent solutions. Turn disruption into leadership advantage. Career Studio AI, EZRA AI coaching, General Assembly skilling. 45%+ of revenue from senior roles.
Akkodis (€3.3 bn), digital engineering. From idea to impact, engineering transformation at speed. Akkodis Intelligence + AI Core. 100% engineers AI-trained. Aerospace, defence, automotive, life sciences.

SIX SELLING MODELS
1. MSP, Pontoon. $14 bn managed spend, NPS 65, nine-time Everest PEAK Matrix Leader, Agentic AI MSP platform. Adecco-owned brand. 2. RPO, Pontoon. Same platform as MSP. Same credentials. Agentic AI applied to permanent hire at scale.
3. VMS, Vendor Management System. Layer that sits under MSP for vendor visibility, spend control, compliance.
4. TSC, Talent Supply Chain. Industrialised delivery, locally tailored, at any scale. Powered by the OneVoice AI engine that transcribes 15M candidate calls per year.
5. Direct sourcing. Programmes that build employer-owned candidate pools, often layered with MSP.
6. rPotential JV. Salesforce joint venture led by ex-Adecco SVP Greg Shewmaker. Launched January 2026. Separate brand, NOT a TAG-branded service. SEVEN SOLUTIONS
1. Flexible placement, temporary and contingent staffing.
2. Outsourcing master, RPO, BPO, MSP, MVP, Onsite under one heading.
3. Outsourcing portfolio catalogue, the canonical list of every Adecco Outsourcing service.
4. Outsourcing solution features, feature matrix with shared-vs-Adecco-overall responsibility flags. SOW-level contract logic.
5. Outsourcing case library, reference cases per industry.
6. Permanent recruitment, full-time hire programmes.
7. Adecco Career Center, candidate-side career portal.

FOUR DELIVERY MODELS
1. Branch network, 5,000 offices Group-wide (Adecco branches plus Pontoon, Akkodis, LHH locations). 3,800 is the Adecco-only count.
2. Onsite solutions, account team embedded at the client site.
3. Global hub, centralised delivery for multi-country accounts.
4. Digital Adecco, self-service candidate and client channels.

GDM OPERATING MODEL, TEN TOPICS
KPIs, SLAs, reporting. Cost savings. Compliance. Diversity and inclusion. Health and safety. Data protection and security. Global governance. Business continuity. Payroll (multi-country, with Everest PEAK matrix benchmarks). Pixid tech platform.

FIVE INDUSTRY PLAYS
1. Automotive, ACEA Race to Reskill. TAG authored the executive summary. Industry authority for any automotive OEM pitch.
2. Logistics. Logistics newsletter and Geodis-RFP context.
3. Airport services. Industry deck.
4. Akkodis marketing dashboard 2025.
5. Volvo Cars BlueCollar case. Concrete customer case.
