Privacy policy.

Effective date: 2026-06-02 · Operated by the Adecco Group, Global Digital Marketing

What we collect

The Adecco Group UTM Builder helps employees generate tracking links for campaigns. We collect and process:

  1. Account data (if you create an account): username, work email, full name, password hash. Stored on the Adecco Group's controlled infrastructure.
  2. Link metadata (when you build a link): destination URL, UTM parameters (source, medium, campaign, term, content, id), brand and country, additional dimensions, generated short URL code, your account email as the link owner.
  3. Click data (when someone clicks one of the short links): timestamp, IP address, browser User-Agent, referring page URL. Used to count clicks and aggregate reporting.
  4. Browser extension: the URL of your currently active tab is read only when you click the extension icon, and only sent to the backend when you click "Generate link".

What we don't collect

  • No third-party analytics or trackers
  • No telemetry sent to external services
  • No data shared beyond what you explicitly produce (your generated link, and the visitor's click data on it)

How we use it

  • Account data: to authenticate you and attribute your links
  • Link metadata: to provide the link-building service, audit trail, admin approval workflow
  • Click data: to provide aggregate metrics in the Performance Dashboard
  • All data processed on Adecco Group-controlled infrastructure

Retention

  • Account data: kept while your account is active. Deletion via admin request.
  • Link metadata: kept indefinitely as marketing campaign record. Individual links can be deleted.
  • Click data: detailed for 90 days, then aggregated.

Your rights (GDPR)

You can request access, correction, or deletion via the admin (johannes.schatt@adeccogroup.com). External visitors can request deletion of their click data by emailing the same address with the short link code in question.

Lawful basis

Employees: legitimate interest in operating internal marketing tools, balanced with employee rights.
Visitors: legitimate interest in measuring marketing effectiveness, with data minimization (IPs partially anonymized after 30 days).

Updates

This policy may be updated. Material changes will be announced via in-app notice. The current version is always at {{ base }}/privacy.

Contact
